Software Engineer — Crunchyroll, LLC — Hyderabad, Telangana, India (Hybrid: 2 days office, 3 days WFH)
Team: Service Monetization

About the role
As a Software Engineer on the Service Monetization Team, you will help shape the future of our
Service Monetization Platform. You will architect, build and optimize our subscriptions and payments
platform that enables membership and powers revenue collection for Crunchyroll. Working with design,
product, quality and data teams, you will solve complex challenges, optimize performance, and enhance
user experience, directly contributing to the growth of the Crunchyroll fanbase.

About You
- Experienced and passionate Software Engineer with experience building robust and reliable backend
  services (2+ years of relevant experience in the software industry)
- Experienced in Java, GoLang, Python, PHP, Typescript or Node.js
- Experienced with cloud technologies (API Gateway, Lambda, EC2, RDS, ECS)
- Experienced with databases (PostgresDB/MariaDB, DynamoDB/MongoDB)
- Experienced in operational excellence (on-call rotations, incident management)
- Excel in an Agile and collaborative environment

About the Team
The Service Monetization organization is responsible for building, maintaining, and securing systems
that enable membership and collect payments information for Crunchyroll users. We are directly tied to
many streams of revenue and pride ourselves on quality code, service reliability, and teamwork. Our day
to day work includes integrations with payment providers, payments fraud detection and prevention,
optimizations to reduce passive churn while maintaining PCI compliance. Our services process millions of
payments in a highly secure, distributed, and scalable environment that is constantly evolving to meet
the needs of both our fans and growing Crunchyroll business.
