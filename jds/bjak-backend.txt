Backend Engineer — BJAK — Remote (candidates must be based in India)
Team: AI Neobank / core backend

About BJAK
Started in 2019, BJAK built the first mobile-first insurance platform in Southeast Asia and is now
the leading insurance platform in the region. It is expanding into spending, saving, investing,
exchanging and travel. Mission: help people get more from their money every day. Teams work
remotely across 20+ nationalities.

The Role
Build the core systems behind BJAK's AI Neobank app: reliable APIs, workflows and financial
product infrastructure that scale across markets, partners and millions of users.

What You'll Own
- Build backend services and APIs for onboarding, accounts, payments, cards, transfers, rewards,
  travel, FX, KYC and risk flows.
- Design reliable systems supporting real customer transactions, operational workflows and future
  financial products.
- Integrate with internal systems, financial partners, payment providers, AI services and
  third-party platforms.
- Own backend quality across reliability, security, latency, observability, data integrity and
  incident handling.
- Work with product, frontend, mobile and AI teams to launch features quickly and safely.
- Improve existing systems by simplifying flows, removing manual work and automating repeatable
  processes.

What We're Looking For
- Strong backend engineering experience in production systems.
- Good understanding of APIs, databases, authentication, authorization, queues, jobs, events and
  integrations.
- Experience building services used by real customers or internal operations at meaningful scale.
- Strong debugging, reliability, security and data quality mindset.
- Able to make practical trade-offs between speed, correctness, scalability and maintainability.
- Fintech, payments, banking, wallet, KYC, fraud or risk experience is a strong plus, but not
  required if fundamentals are strong.

This Role Is Not For
- Engineers who only want isolated backend tasks.
- People who ignore security, data consistency, monitoring or operational risk.
- Developers who cannot work with fast-changing product requirements.
- Candidates who talk about scale but have not handled production systems.
- People who exaggerate impact without being clear on ownership.

The Bar
Engineers with real technology career highlights — personally helped build, ship or scale a
serious product, platform or system.

Location: Remote, must already be based in India.
Language: English is the main working language; strong English communication required.
Interview process: online assessment or practical task, technical interview, CEO / final round.
